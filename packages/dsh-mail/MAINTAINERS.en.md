Use [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/ecnu/EduWork/security/advisories/new) for confidential security reports and [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ecnu/EduWork/issues) for release-recovery requests that contain no sensitive information. Recruiting at least one backup able to handle private reports and release recovery remains a maintenance recommendation and roadmap item. Until then, the project does not claim backup coverage or institutional support. Credential, send-authorization, workspace-boundary, and release-workflow changes should receive independent review whenever possible.

Role responsibilities and review thresholds are defined in [Governance](GOVERNANCE.en.md).
