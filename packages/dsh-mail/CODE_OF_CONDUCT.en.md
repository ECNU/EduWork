# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free project.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas, protocols, and code rather than personal characteristics;
- assume good intent while accepting evidence-based correction;
- protect private security reports, mail data, account configuration, and personal information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption;
- respect maintainer decisions made to protect users, the mail boundary, licensing, or supply-chain security.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens contributors or users. Measures should be proportionate and avoid exposing affected people or security-sensitive material.

General conduct concerns may use an issue without personal information. For confidential harassment, threats, doxxing, or another incident, use GitHub [Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/ecnu/EduWork/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with “Code of Conduct”.
