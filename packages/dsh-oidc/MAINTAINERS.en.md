The project is currently in its initial single-maintainer phase. It has no backup and does not claim a second human approval. The maintainer explicitly decides each release after recording independent technical review, CI/regression evidence, known limitations, and recovery steps. Adding a backup capable of handling private security reports and release recovery is a future governance goal; the roster and repository permissions must be updated when one is added.

Role responsibilities and review thresholds are defined in [`GOVERNANCE.en.md`](GOVERNANCE.en.md).
