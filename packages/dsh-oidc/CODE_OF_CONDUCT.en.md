# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | **English**

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free project.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and code rather than personal characteristics;
- assume good intent while accepting evidence-based correction;
- protect private security reports, institutional information, and personal data;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption;
- respect maintainer decisions made to protect users, protocol stability, licensing, or security.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens contributors or users. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid disclosing victims or security-sensitive details.

General conduct concerns may use an issue that contains no personal or sensitive details. For harassment, threats, doxxing, or another incident requiring confidentiality, use GitHub [Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/ecnu/EduWork/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with “Code of Conduct”. Do not disclose an affected person's identity or evidence attachments publicly.
